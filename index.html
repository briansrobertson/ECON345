What you'll learn

The course gives you the foundational data preparation and analysis skills needed to perform or support research in economics, finance, and related fields, using R.

  • Reading, cleaning, and merging data sets
  • Identifying and handling outliers and other data issues
  • Creating tables and charts to summarize data
  • Analyzing and communicating relationships between variables
  • How economic and statistical tools support descriptive analysis

Prerequisite: Econ 303 Econometrics with a grade of B or higher. No prior programming or data science training required.

Grading

65%
Final project

Rough draft, in-class presentation, and final paper (45%), plus six graded project updates (20%). Students work with an Atlanta Fed economist mentor and a project buddy. Without the final project a student will not pass the course.

20%
Weekly homework

Posted to Canvas, generally due Thursday nights by midnight. Lecture homework and project homework are often due at the same time, so check the schedule carefully. If you turn in every homework reasonably completed, your lowest grade is dropped.

10%
Quizzes & attendance

A short quiz starts every class, 9:00 to 9:10am sharp. Unexcused absence or lateness earns a zero with no make-up, though your lowest quiz is dropped. A shorter, ungraded exit ticket closes each class.

5%
Communication & professionalism

Coming prepared to class and to meetings with your project buddy and mentor, giving one to two business days' notice when scheduling or canceling, and using respectful, professional language with course staff.

Late and incomplete policy

Late submissions are reduced by 2 percentage points for every partial hour late. An assignment earning 85% submitted 2 hours and 20 minutes late receives a 6 point deduction, for a final score of 79%.

Because the research project relies on the generosity and time of mentors and project buddies, Incompletes are not granted. A letter grade is assigned at the end of the semester regardless of circumstances.

Absences

Request an excused absence in advance by emailing econ345.spelman@gmail.com. You are responsible for taking the make-up quiz within seven days of the missed class, normally during office hours.

Even if your absence is unexcused, let us know you will miss class so we are not waiting for you at the security entrance.

Academic integrity

Per the Spelman College Academic Integrity Policy, you may work collaboratively but each person writes up their own solutions. Think and work through every question together; do not divide and conquer.

Cite all sources on your project, and write everything in your own words, even a summary of another paper.

How much work to expect

Plan on an hour outside class for every hour in lecture: roughly three hours a week studying, reviewing, and attending office hours.

Turning in homework late during the first three weeks, or missing two of the first five classes, will seriously jeopardize your ability to pass the class.
